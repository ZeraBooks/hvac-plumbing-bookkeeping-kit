# Month-end checklist: HVAC or plumbing company

For residential service and replacement work on accrual books. Work top to
bottom. The deposit and plan steps come before the profit review because
they decide what counts as this month's revenue.

Month: ____________  Closed by: ____________  Date locked: ____________

## 1. Get everything in
- [ ] Bank and card statements for the full month, as PDFs
- [ ] Invoice and payments report from your field service software
- [ ] Supply house statements for every account
- [ ] Financing payout reports from each lending partner
- [ ] Payroll report for every pay run in the month
- [ ] Van lease and loan statements

## 2. Service and installs
- [ ] Service and repair invoices in service and repair revenue
- [ ] Finished installs in installation revenue, at full price
- [ ] Revenue in the books agrees with the field service invoice report

## 3. Install deposits
- [ ] Deposits taken this month added to customer deposits
- [ ] Deposits on jobs finished this month moved into revenue with the final
      invoice
- [ ] Customer deposits balance agrees with the list of open jobs that have
      deposits

## 4. Maintenance agreements
- [ ] Plans sold this month added to deferred maintenance revenue
- [ ] This month's share of every open plan moved into maintenance agreement
      revenue
- [ ] Deferred balance agrees with the plan schedule

## 5. Financed jobs
- [ ] Each financed job booked at full price, with the dealer fee as a
      financing fee
- [ ] Each lender payout matched to its jobs; finished jobs not yet paid out
      listed

## 6. Parts and truck stock
- [ ] Supply house invoices split between parts used on jobs and parts that
      went onto the vans
- [ ] Truck stock counted or estimated, and the asset adjusted
- [ ] Each supply house statement ties to accounts payable for that vendor
- [ ] Callbacks and warranty parts coded to their own account

## 7. Payroll from the report
- [ ] Technician wages in cost of goods sold, office wages in overhead, both
      from the payroll report
- [ ] Employer taxes and withholdings booked from the report

## 8. Reconcile and review
- [ ] Every bank and card account ties to its printed closing balance
- [ ] Sales tax payable = tax collected - tax remitted
- [ ] Profit by service and installs compared with last month
- [ ] Uncategorized at zero

## 9. Lock and send
- [ ] Month locked so a late edit can't change it
- [ ] Reports sent to the owner, with open questions listed
